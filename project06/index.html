Let's Make a Deal!

How to Play:

  1. You will be presented with three doors, each hiding a different cash prize.
  2. Choose one door using your mouse or keyboard. Your prize will remain hidden.
  3. After you make your selection, the game will make you a cash offer to give up your chosen door.
  4. You can accept the cash offer, keep your original door, or switch to another door if given the option.
  5. Once you make your final decision, all three prizes will be revealed.
  6. The goal is to win as much money as possible, so choose wisely!
